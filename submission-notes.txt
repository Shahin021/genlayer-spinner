Quorum — a loading spinner for the Portal. One self-contained SVG, CSS-animated: no JS, no runtime, no assets.

Identity: I rasterised the official mark and traced it: outer edges at dx/dy 0.482 (25.7° off vertical), the apex channel, the notch, the core kite. Each blade is split once, perpendicular to its axis, into two equal-area parts; with the core, five. The official mark asset is never modified or animated; this is a separate component derived from its geometry.

Motion: each settled state resolves to a 3-of-5 majority. Every 320 ms one seat joins, one leaves, three hold; 1.6 s seamless loop. Opacity is the only animated property; reduced motion holds one settled state.

Colour: only Cobalt, Void and Ceramic Node; others are a color-mix() of those three. Dark accent #5554FC is 70/30 Cobalt/Ceramic — 3.9:1 on Void, where full Cobalt is 2.4:1.

Verified 16–48 px on both surfaces. Inline + CSS variables for theming; fixed light/dark variants for <img>.
